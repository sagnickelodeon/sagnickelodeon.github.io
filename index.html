OSM Mapping Surge Tracker

Detecting disasters and humanitarian crises from spikes in OpenStreetMap edits — in real time.

When disaster strikes — a flood in Karnataka, a quake in Türkiye — volunteers flood OpenStreetMap with new buildings and roads within hours. This system watches worldwide OSM edits and flags any region breaking from its own 7-day, hour-of-day baseline, surfacing each surge on a live map with an AI-generated explanation and related news.
